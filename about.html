About.

Ganesh Patel and the photography approach behind Big Boss Photography.

Ganesh Patel

Creative direction with
a personal touch.

Big Boss Photography was built around a simple idea: photographs should look refined while still feeling like the people in them.

Based in Petlad, Anand, the studio covers weddings, pre-weddings, modelling portfolios, baby showers, portraits and bio-data sessions. Each project begins with understanding the occasion, the people involved and the style they want.

Professional cameras, lighting, drone coverage and video equipment are used where the project requires them, but the real focus is always timing, comfort and attention to detail.
